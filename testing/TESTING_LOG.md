# Testing and benchmark record

This directory is the append-oriented index of test and benchmark executions. Store each run's date, commit, platform/runtime versions, exact command, outcome, and skips. Detailed benchmark measurements belong with their benchmark artifacts under `benchmarks/` or in a dated report under `docs/`.

Do not replace an older result with a newer one; append a new row. Do not label a skipped or unrun test as passing. Historical reports elsewhere in the repository are retained and should be interpreted using their recorded date and environment.
